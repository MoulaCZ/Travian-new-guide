# Alliance & Diplomacy

An alliance is the backbone of competitive Travian play. Solo players almost never win servers — the game is fundamentally built around cooperation.

---

## Why Join an Alliance

| Benefit | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Protection** | Large players in your alliance deter attackers |
| **Reinforcement** | Alliance members can send troops to defend you |
| **Resources** | Emergency resource sharing in crises |
| **Information** | Intel on dangerous neighbors, war targets |
| **Endgame** | Required to seriously compete for WW |

> 💡 Join an alliance **within your first 2 days**. Being unaffiliated early is dangerous and leaves you without the most important resource in the game — other players.

---

## Alliance Roles

Most alliances have defined roles for players:

| Role | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Founder/Leader** | Admin access, manages alliance |
| **Diplomat** | Handles NAPs and war declarations |
| **Military coordinator** | Plans attacks and defense |
| **Hammer player** | Builds and maintains a large offensive army |
| **Anvil player** | Builds and maintains a large defensive army |
| **Feeder** | Produces resources to support hammer players |

> 💡 Ask your alliance what **role they need you to fill** early. Playing the wrong role wastes time and resources.

---

## Alliance Structure

Large alliances often consist of:
- **Main alliance** — top active players, strict requirements
- **Wing alliance** — second tier, feeders, newer players
- **Academy alliance** — training ground for absolute beginners

If you're new, you'll likely start in a wing or academy alliance and get promoted as you grow.

---

## Diplomacy

### Non-Aggression Pact (NAP)
An agreement between two alliances not to attack each other. Benefits both sides — lets you focus on other enemies.

### War
Formal declaration of conflict. Both alliances can openly attack each other. Wars are usually announced in alliance forum/Discord.

### Confederation
Two alliances cooperate closely — shared intelligence, coordinated attacks, sometimes resource sharing.

### Protectorate
A larger alliance protects a smaller one in exchange for loyalty/resource support.

---

## Alliance Communication

Good alliances use **external communication** (Discord, Telegram, or forum) because in-game messaging is slow and limited.

**What to communicate:**
- "I'm being attacked, need reinforcement"
- Scouting reports on enemies
- Planned attack timing and targets
- Resource needs
- Daily/weekly status updates

> ⚠️ Failing to communicate is the #1 reason new players get kicked from alliances. If you're being attacked, **tell your alliance immediately**.

---

## Reinforcing Allies

When an ally needs defense:
1. Go to **Rally Point**
2. Select troops to send
3. Enter ally's village coordinates
4. Select **"Reinforcement"** as mission type
5. Send

- Your troops defend the allied village while there
- You still consume the crop (your troops, your cost)
- If the village is lost (conquered), your troops return home

> 💡 Before sending reinforcement, check if the attacker is much stronger — sending troops into a lost village just loses them.

## Endgame Alliance Play

### Pre-endgame preparation (months 3–5)
- Identify who will be the **WW holder** (usually the alliance's strongest player)
- Coordinate **artefact captures** — alliances race to grab artefacts when they appear
- Assign roles: hammers, anvils, feeders

### During WW phase
- **WW holder** builds the Wonder — needs massive resource support
- **Hammers** attack enemy WWs to set them back
- **Anvils** reinforce the WW and key alliance villages
- **All members** trade resources to the WW holder regularly

### Intelligence
- Scouts of enemy WWs weekly
- Track enemy hammer movements (sudden troop disappearances = hammer launched)
- Use Forum/Discord to share reports

---

## Tips for New Players in an Alliance

1. **Be active** — log in at least twice a day, check messages
2. **Respond quickly** — if you're asked for reinforcement, reply even if you can't help
3. **Share reports** — if you scout something interesting, post it in Discord
4. **Ask questions** — experienced players are usually happy to help newer members
5. **Don't go rogue** — attacking someone the alliance has a NAP with can cause diplomatic disaster
6. **Grow consistently** — being the weakest, least-developed member of an alliance is a burden; focus on your economy
