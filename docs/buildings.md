# Buildings

Each village can contain up to 22 building slots (plus 18 resource fields). Managing what you build and when is critical for efficient development.

---

## Building Queue

- You can queue multiple buildings, but they build **one at a time**
- **Romans** can build and train troops simultaneously (unique advantage)
- Upgrading the **Main Building** speeds up all construction

## Military Buildings

| Building | Purpose | Notes |
|----------|---------|-------|
| **Barracks** | Train infantry | Build 2–3 for faster training (Teuton strategy) |
| **Stable** | Train cavalry | Requires Academy + Barracks |
| **Workshop** | Train siege weapons (Rams, Catapults) | Requires level 10+ Main Building |
| **Smithy** | Upgrade unit stats | Each level = stronger troops |
| **Academy** | Research units | Required before training new unit types |
| **Rally Point** | Troop management, farm list | Required for coordinated attacks |
| **Wall** (nation-specific) | Defense bonus | Always max this in defensive villages |

## Building Priority by Phase

### Early Game
1. Main Building → level 5
2. Warehouse + Granary → level 3
3. Barracks → level 1 (start training immediately)
4. Marketplace → level 1
5. Rally Point → level 1
6. Academy → level 1 (research your nation's better infantry)

### Mid Game
7. Main Building → level 10+
8. Stable → level 1 (cavalry)
9. Smithy → level 5+ (upgrade your primary unit)
10. Hero's Mansion → level 10 (first oasis slot)
11. Workshop → level 1 (Rams for attacks)
12. Residence/Palace → level 1 (prepare for second village)

### Late Game
13. Treasury → required for artefacts
14. Great Barracks/Stable → in your capital/army village
15. Workshop → level 20 (max catapult research)

## Main Building Levels — Construction Speed

| Level | Speed bonus |
|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Baseline |
| 5 | ~25% faster |
| 10 | ~50% faster |
| 15 | ~75% faster |
| 20 | ~100% faster (builds twice as fast) |

> 💡 Upgrading Main Building from level 1 to 5 is one of the best early investments — it pays back quickly through faster construction of everything else.

---

## Tips

- **Don't build everything** — each building takes up a slot. Be selective.
- **Demolishing buildings** is possible but slow — plan before you build.
- **Multiple Warehouses** are valid — sometimes easier than upgrading one to level 20.
- In your **army village**, prioritize Barracks, Stable, Smithy, and Granary over everything else.
- **Great Barracks/Stable** are only worth it in a dedicated army village (3x training speed costs 3x crop — not sustainable everywhere).
