# Choosing Defensive Units

> This page covers **Teutons, Gauls, and Romans** only — the three classic tribes. The goal is to maximise DEF output per hour, not to collect every unit type.

---

## Core Principle

Your job as a defender is to **maximise DEF per hour**. That means picking units that are:
- **Cheap to produce** (more units per resource)
- **Fast to train** (shorter queue = more units)
- **Good at their role** (anti-infantry or anti-cavalry)

A mixed wall (infantry + cavalry) is stronger than a pure one — infantry fills the anti-cav gap, cavalry responds fast. But the ratio matters: **don't split evenly**. Bulk up on infantry, add cavalry for speed.

> The exact unit mix across your alliance is coordinated by your **def lead** — they balance anti-infantry vs. anti-cavalry across the whole wall. Follow their direction.

---

## Teutons

**Recommended mix: 75% Spearman / 25% Paladin**

| Unit | Role | Why |
|------|------|-----|
| Spearman | Anti-cavalry (infantry) | Cheap, fast to train, excellent anti-cav DEF |
| Paladin | Anti-infantry (cavalry) | Slow but strong against infantry; can also raid between calls |

- Spearmen are the backbone — train them non-stop in Barracks
- Paladins add speed to your wall and can be used for raids when not on call
- Teutons have one of the cheapest DEF productions in the game — volume is your strength

---

## Gauls

**Recommended mix: 70% Phalanx / 30% Druidrider**

| Unit | Role | Why |
|------|------|-----|
| Phalanx | Anti-cavalry (infantry) | Strong anti-cav, fast to rebuild after losses |
| Druidrider | Anti-infantry (cavalry) | One of the best speedy cavalry defenders in the game |

- Phalanx rebuilds fast — Gauls recover from losses better than other tribes
- Druidriders are the go-to fast response unit; they cover the anti-infantry gap
- Strong all-round tribe for DEF; great for players who expect to take hits

---

## Romans

**Recommended: Praetorian (primary) or Legionnaire (flexible)**

| Unit | Role | Why |
|------|------|-----|
| Praetorian | Anti-infantry (infantry) | Best infantry defender in the game |
| Legionnaire | Flexible (infantry) | Weaker than Preto for pure DEF, but usable offensively too |

- Romans are the **slowest classic DEF tribe** — slower troop speed means slower response to calls
- Compensate with a high-level **Tournament Square** to close the speed gap
- **No cavalry needed** — Praetorians alone make an extremely strong wall
- If you want flexibility (raids, small hammer), mix in some Legionnaires — but pure Pretos are stronger for DEF

---

## General Rules

1. **Infantry first, cavalry second.** Infantry is cheaper per hour of production; always bulk up there first.
2. **Rough ratio: 3–4 Barracks for every 1 Stable.** Don't over-invest in cavalry production.
3. **Never idle the queue.** An empty Barracks or Stable is wasted time you can't get back.
4. **Skip Great Barracks and Great Stables.** For the same cost, run 3 normal Barracks across 3 villages and triple your output.
5. **Research and smith your units.** Higher smithy levels = better stats = fewer losses per hit.
