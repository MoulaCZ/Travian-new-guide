# Early Game (Days 1–14)

The early game covers the **Beginner's Protection phase** and the days immediately after. Your goals are: stable economy, first troops, joined alliance, and survival after protection ends.

---

## Phase 1: Beginner's Protection (Days 1–~3)

### Goals
- Max out resource fields to at least level 1 (all four: wood, clay, iron, crop)
- Complete all tutorial quests
- Join an alliance
- Train first troops

### Quest priority
Always do your **quest chain** — it gives free resources, speed-ups, and sometimes free buildings. This is the single most impactful thing you can do in hour 1.

### Resource fields — upgrade order
1. Upgrade all fields to level 1 first (balanced income)
2. Then focus on **your highest-value fields** (check which resources you need most)
3. Always keep an eye on **crop** — negative crop = troops starve and die

> 💡 A common mistake: forgetting crop fields. You can have full warehouses and still lose troops if granary is empty or crop production is negative.

### First buildings (priority order)
1. **Main Building** → upgrade to level 3–5 (speeds up all building construction)
2. **Warehouse** → upgrade to level 3+ (you'll overflow resources otherwise)
3. **Granary** → upgrade to level 3+
4. **Barracks** → build and start training troops immediately
5. **Marketplace** → needed for trading

---

## Phase 2: After Protection Ends (Days 3–7)

Now you're vulnerable. Priorities shift.

### Defense first
- Have **at least 20–50 troops** in your village before protection ends
- Gauls: fill your Trapper with traps
- Teutons: your troops should already be out raiding — keep some home
- Romans: Praetorians are cheap and strong defenders

### Raiding
Even if you're not a Teuton, you should raid:
- Look for **inactive/abandoned villages** on the map (grey names = potential farms)
- Scout before attacking if you can afford scouts
- Start with small raids — 5–10 units — to test if villages are empty
- Use **Farm List** (Reports → Farm List) to organize and automate repeat raids

### Alliance
If you haven't joined an alliance yet — do it now. An active alliance means:
- Someone will reinforce you if you're attacked
- You'll get advice on what to build
- Shared intelligence on dangerous neighbors

---

## Phase 3: Stabilization (Days 7–14)

### Economy targets by Day 14
| Resource | Production target (per hour) |
|----------|------------------------------|
| Wood | 200+ |
| Clay | 200+ |
| Iron | 200+ |
| Crop | 150+ (net positive after troops) |

### Building priorities
- **Academy** → research better units (Imperian for Romans, Axeman for Teutons, Swordsman for Gauls)
- **Smithy** → start upgrading your main offensive unit (attack bonus)
- **Rally Point** → needed for farm list and attack coordination
- **Hero's Mansion** → level it up to send your hero on adventures for extra resources

### Hero
Don't neglect your **Hero**:
- Send on adventures regularly (passive loot)
- Equip better items as you find them
- Level up combat stats if you plan to raid
- Hero can carry extra resources when returning from attacks

## Key Numbers to Track

| Metric | What it means | Warning sign |
|--------|--------------|--------------|
| **Population** | Reflects village development | Below 100 by day 7 = you're behind |
| **Crop balance** | Production minus consumption | Negative = troops starving |
| **Troops trained** | Army size | 0 troops = you're a free farm |
| **Warehouse fill** | How full your storage is | Always full = upgrade or build more |

---

## Common Early Game Mistakes

| Mistake | Why it's bad | Fix |
|---------|-------------|-----|
| Ignoring quests | Missing free resources and speed-ups | Do quests first every session |
| No troops before protection ends | You become a free farm | Always have 30+ troops |
| Negative crop | Troops die silently | Check crop production daily |
| Not joining alliance | No help when attacked | Join within first 2 days |
| Upgrading all buildings equally | Slow Main Building = slow everything | Prioritize Main Building first |
| Attacking a player and ending protection early | You're not ready | Only attack NPCs/inactives during protection |

## End of Early Game Checklist

Before moving to mid-game, verify:

- [ ] All resource fields at level 3+
- [ ] Main Building at level 5+
- [ ] Warehouse + Granary upgraded
- [ ] 50+ troops in village
- [ ] Alliance joined
- [ ] Farm list with 5+ inactive targets
- [ ] Academy built, better unit researched
- [ ] Hero equipped and leveled
