# Feeder Village (Mid-Game Village)

> This guide applies roughly to your **3rd–7th village**. By this point you should have a feel for which resources you're running short on and which you're producing in excess — use that knowledge to pick the right village tile.

---

## Choosing the Right Tile

Don't default to a standard **4-4-4-6** valley. By this stage you know your bottleneck resource — find a tile that leans into it.

**Examples:**
- Short on wood? Look for **5-3-4-6** or **5-4-3-6**
- Short on clay? Look for **3-5-4-6** or **4-5-3-6**
- Short on iron? Look for **3-4-5-6** or **4-3-5-6**

**Location priorities:**
1. Close to your existing villages (shorter merchant travel time)
2. Has **oases matching your 5-field resource** — a 25% or 50% oasis on your strongest resource type compounds significantly
3. [Official guide on resource village development](https://support.travian.com/en/support/solutions/articles/7000094130-developing-resource-villages)

---

## Build Strategy for the 5th Village and Beyond

For later villages, prioritize **Main Building → level 20 first** before anything else.

Why: every subsequent building and field upgrade is faster. The investment pays back immediately.

**How to handle the constraints:**
- You'll need to expand at least one **cropland** to avoid negative crop while the MB levels up
- Upgrade **Warehouse** and **Granary** just enough to fit the incoming resources
- **Send resources from your other villages** via trade routes — don't wait for this village to be self-sufficient
- Alternatively, send your **hero** (if you've switched points to resource production) to accelerate early income

---

## Parties: Large vs. Small

Before queuing parties, calculate whether **Large Celebrations** are more efficient than Small ones for your current resource situation.

Compare: resources cost vs. culture points gained vs. time. If large parties give better CP per resource — run large parties exclusively.
