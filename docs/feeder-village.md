# Feeder Village (Mid-Game Village)

---

## Choosing the Right Tile

Don't default to a standard **4-4-4-6** valley. By this stage you know your bottleneck resource — find a tile that leans into it.

**Examples:**
- Short on wood? Look for **5-3-4-6** or **5-4-3-6**
- Short on clay? Look for **3-5-4-6** or **4-5-3-6**
- Short on iron? Look for **3-4-5-6** or **4-3-5-6**

**Location priorities:**
1. Close to your existing villages (shorter merchant travel time)
2. Has **oases matching your 5-field resource** — a 25% or 50% oasis on your strongest resource type compounds significantly

## 2nd Village — Early Feeder

> This guide applies to your **2nd village** (and roughly up to your 4th). It's not yet a pure resource village — it needs its own CP infrastructure and will help feed your main.

### Phase 1 — Main Building to Level 20 first

**Why:** Every subsequent building and field upgrade becomes faster. The investment pays back immediately.

Starej se o tyto věci, dokud MB roste:
- Postav **Warehouse** a **Granary** jen na tolik, aby se ti suroviny vejely
- Rozšiř alespoň jedno **Cropland**, aby tě zásobování nekrátilo při růstu MB
- **Posílej suroviny z ostatních vesnic** přes trh — nečekej na soběstačnost
- Pokud máš hrdinu s body do výroby surovin, pošli ho sem pomáhat

### Phase 2 — Final Build (order matters)

Once MB is at 20, build in this order. **Always watch the ad** for 25% faster builds.

| Priority | Building | Target level | Why |
|----------|----------|--------------|-----|
| 1 | Main Building | 20 | Faster everything |
| 2 | Rally Point | 20 | Cheap CP |
| 3 | Academy | 20 | High CP gain |
| 4 | Embassy | 20 | High CP gain |
| 5 | Marketplace | 20 | CP + trade capacity |
| 6 | Trade Office | 5 | Faster merchants |
| 7 | Town Hall | 13 | Parties → CP |
| 8 | Stable | 20 | CP |
| 9 | Warehouse | 20 | Storage |
| 10 | Granary | 20 | Storage |
| 11 | Residence | 10 | Needed if sending settlers later |
| 12 | Hero's Mansion | 10 | Oasis slots |
| 13 | Palisade / Wall | 10 | Defence |
| 14 | Workshop | 1 | Slot filler |
| 15 | Resource buildings (Mill, Bakery, Sawmill, Brickyard, Iron Foundry) | 4–5 | +% production boost |

### Resource Fields

Develop fields in parallel with buildings — don't neglect them:
1. All fields → **Level 8** (keep balanced)
2. Individual fields → **Level 10** (start with the resource type you need most; resource production buildings should be at L3 by now)
3. Once all fields are at 10 → push the 5-field resource type to the cap

**Total: 22/22 building slots** — plan before building.

## 3rd and 4th Village — Pure Resource Village

> For your 3rd village and beyond, the goal is pure resource output. No military, minimal infrastructure — just fields, storage, and enough CP buildings to function.

Based on the [Official guide on resource village development](https://support.travian.com/en/support/solutions/articles/7000094130-developing-resource-villages).

### Tips before you settle

- Stay online when settlers arrive — start building immediately
- Use hero inventory resources to kickstart the village
- **Always watch the ad** — 25% faster builds from day one
- Queue a long build before you log off

### Phase 1 — Initial Infrastructure (12 steps)

| Step | What |
|------|------|
| 1 | Main Building → 10 |
| 2 | All Resource Fields → 2 |
| 3 | Rally Point → 1 |
| 4 | One Cropland → 5 (to avoid starvation) |
| 5 | Warehouse → 3 |
| 6 | Granary → 2 |
| 7 | Marketplace → 1 |
| 8 | Residence / Command Centre → 1 |
| 9 | Main Building → 14 |
| 10 | Warehouse → 8 |
| 11 | Granary → 3 |
| 12 | Main Building → 20 |

### Phase 2 — Resource Field Development

Once infrastructure is in place:

1. All fields → **Level 8** (balanced growth)
2. Individual fields → **Level 10** (resource buildings at L3; start with your 5-field type)
3. All fields → **Level 9** → then **Level 10**
4. Resource production buildings (Sawmill, Brickyard, Iron Foundry, Mill, Bakery) → **Level 5**

### Phase 3 — CP Infrastructure (when you hit 250–350 CP/day)

Once the village generates solid resources, add buildings that give culture points:

| Building | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| Rally Point | Cheap CP |
| Marketplace | CP + merchants |
| Palisade / Wall | Defence + CP |
| Academy | High CP |
| Embassy | High CP |
| Town Hall | Run small celebrations for CP |
| Trade Office | Faster merchant trips |

> A well-developed resource village can reach **~1,000 CP/day** and still produce a surplus of resources to ship to your capital or military villages.
