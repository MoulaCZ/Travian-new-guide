# Feeder Village (Mid-Game Village)

> This guide covers your **3rd–7th village**. By this point you should have a feel for which resources you're running short on — use that to pick the right tile.

---

## Choosing the Right Tile

Don't default to a standard **4-4-4-6** valley. Find a tile that covers your bottleneck resource.

**Examples:**
- Short on wood? Look for **5-3-4-6** or **5-4-3-6**
- Short on clay? Look for **3-5-4-6** or **4-5-3-6**
- Short on iron? Look for **3-4-5-6** or **4-3-5-6**

**Location priorities:**
1. Close to your existing villages (shorter merchant travel time)
2. Has **oases matching your 5-field resource** — a 25% or 50% oasis on your strongest type compounds significantly

**Always watch the ad** before every build — 25% faster construction, free.

## 3rd and 4th Village — Resource Village

> Follow the [Official Travian guide on resource village development](https://support.travian.com/en/support/solutions/articles/7000094130-developing-resource-villages). The goal here is to get resources flowing as fast as possible — not to build everything.

### Tips before you settle

- Stay online when settlers arrive — start building immediately
- Use hero inventory resources to kickstart the village
- Queue a long build before you log off

### Phase 1 — Initial Infrastructure (Not in any particular order, but focus mainly of Main Building)

| Step | What |
|------|------|
| 1 | Main Building → 10 |
| 2 | All Resource Fields → 2 |
| 3 | Rally Point → 1 |
| 4 | One Cropland → 5 (prevents starvation) |
| 5 | Warehouse → 3 |
| 6 | Granary → 2 |
| 7 | Marketplace → 1 |
| 8 | Residence / Command Centre → 1 |
| 9 | Main Building → 14 |
| 10 | Warehouse → 8 |
| 11 | Granary → 3 |

### Phase 2 — Resource Field Development

| Step | What |
|------|------|
| 1 | All fields → **Level 8** (balanced) |
| 2 | Individual fields → **Level 10**, starting with your 5-field resource type (resource buildings at L3 by now) |
| 3 | All fields → **Level 9**, then **Level 10** |
| 4 | Resource buildings (Sawmill, Brickyard, Iron Foundry, Mill, Bakery) → **Level 5** |

### Phase 3 — CP Infrastructure (once you generate 250–350 CP/day)

Add these buildings once the village produces enough resources to spare:

| Building | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| Rally Point | Cheap CP |
| Marketplace | CP + trade capacity |
| Wall / Palisade | Defence + CP |
| Academy | High CP |
| Embassy | High CP |
| Town Hall | Run small celebrations for extra CP |
| Trade Office | Faster merchant trips |

> A fully developed resource village reaches **~1,000 CP/day** and exports a surplus to your capital and military villages.

## 5th Village and Beyond — Late-Game Feeder

> For later villages you can afford a more aggressive strategy: **Main Building to level 20 first**, then build everything else faster.

### Why MB → 20 first?

Every subsequent building and field upgrade is cheaper in time. The investment pays back immediately on a village that will be active for months.

While MB levels up:
- Expand at least one **Cropland** to avoid negative crop
- Upgrade **Warehouse** and **Granary** just enough to hold incoming resources
- **Send resources from other villages** via trade routes — don't wait for self-sufficiency

### Final Build — target state (order matters)

| Priority | Building | Target level | Why |
|----------|----------|--------------|-----|
| 1 | Main Building | 20 | Faster everything |
| 2 | Rally Point | 20 | Cheap CP |
| 3 | Academy | 20 | High CP |
| 4 | Embassy | 20 | High CP |
| 5 | Marketplace | 20 | CP + trade |
| 6 | Trade Office | 5 | Faster merchants |
| 7 | Town Hall | 13 | Parties → CP |
| 8 | Stable | 20 | CP |
| 9 | Warehouse | 20 | Storage |
| 10 | Granary | 20 | Storage |
| 11 | Residence | 10 | Needed if sending settlers later |
| 12 | Hero's Mansion | 10 | Oasis slots |
| 13 | Wall / Palisade | 10 | Defence |
| 14 | Workshop | 1 | Slot filler |
| 15 | Resource buildings (Mill, Bakery, Sawmill, Brickyard, Iron Foundry) | 4–5 | +% production |

### Resource Fields

Same progression as 3rd/4th village, but you can push harder since your other villages are feeding resources in:
1. All fields → **Level 8**
2. Individual fields → **Level 10** (start with the 5-field type)
3. Push all fields to the cap

**Total: 22/22 building slots** — plan before building.

## Parties: Large vs. Small

Before queuing parties, calculate whether **Large Celebrations** are more efficient than Small ones.

Compare: resource cost vs. CP gained vs. time. If large parties give better CP per resource — run large parties exclusively.
