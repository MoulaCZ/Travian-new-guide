# Getting Started

## What is Travian Legends?

Travian Legends is a browser-based strategy game where you build a village, grow resources, train an army, and compete with thousands of other players on a shared map. Each server runs for roughly **12 months**, ending with an **endgame (Wonder of the World)** phase.

This guide focuses on **1x speed servers** — the standard, most common experience.

---

## 1. Choosing Your Nation

There are three nations. Your choice affects your **building costs, unit stats, and playstyle**. There is no "best" nation — each excels in different roles.

| Nation | Playstyle | Difficulty | Best for |
|--------|-----------|------------|----------|
| **Romans** | Balanced, can build + train simultaneously | Medium | New players who want structure |
| **Gauls** | Fast units, excellent defense | Easy | Defensive/support players |
| **Teutons** | Cheap units, aggressive early raiding | Hard | Experienced players, raiders |

> 💡 **Recommendation for first-time players:** Start with **Romans** or **Gauls**. Teutons require aggressive early play and are unforgiving if you fall behind on raiding.

See dedicated guides: [Romans](nations/romans.md) · [Gauls](nations/gauls.md) · [Teutons](nations/teutons.md)

## 2. Starting Location

When you register, the game places you automatically. You **cannot choose your exact coordinates**, but you can influence the region:

- **Selecting a quadrant** (NW, NE, SW, SE) during registration places you in that area.
- Stay within your alliance's area if you already know where they are.

> 💡 Being close to your alliance is one of the most important early decisions.

---

## 3. First 24 Hours — What to Do

Your village starts under **Beginner's Protection** (several days, no one can attack you). Use this time to build your economy.

### Priority order on Day 1:

1. Complete the **tutorial quests** — they give free resources and speed up your start significantly.
2. Build all **resource fields** to at least level 1 (wood, clay, iron, crop).
3. Upgrade **Warehouse** and **Granary** to store more resources.
4. Build a **Marketplace** early (needed for trading with allies).
5. Build a **Barracks** (start training troops before protection ends).

### What NOT to do on Day 1:
- Don't spend resources on decorative or optional buildings.
- Don't ignore crop — running negative crop kills your troops.
- Don't send troops to attack during protection (you can scout safely).

---

## 4. Beginner's Protection

Beginner's Protection lasts a few days at the start. During this time:
- **No one can attack your village** (you are safe).
- **You cannot attack other villages** (only farms/oases).
- It ends automatically after the time limit, or **immediately if you send troops to attack another player**.

> ⚠️ Do not end your protection early by accident. Make sure your village is defended and your economy is running before protection ends.

---

## 5. Joining an Alliance

Join an alliance **as soon as possible** — ideally within the first day or two.

Benefits:
- Protection from larger players
- Shared resources (in emergencies)
- Coordination for attacks and defense
- Access to experienced players who can advise you

How to find an alliance:
1. Go to **Statistics → Alliances** and look at top alliances near your starting area.
2. Send a diplomacy message requesting to join.
3. Or ask in the server's forum/Discord if your region has one.

## 6. Interface Overview

| Section | What it does |
|---------|-------------|
| **Village view** | Your main village — click buildings to upgrade |
| **Map** | The world map — explore neighbors, find farms |
| **Reports** | Attack/defense/scout reports |
| **Messages** | In-game messaging |
| **Statistics** | Rankings for players, alliances, population |
| **Quests** | Daily and story quests — always complete these |
