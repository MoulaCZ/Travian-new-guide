# Gold, Silver & Premium Features

> This guide assumes you are a **F2P (free-to-play) player** — meaning you do not buy Gold with real money. Everything here is achievable without spending a cent, but it requires smart prioritisation.

---

## How to Earn Gold Without Paying

### 1. Refer a Friend
The most valuable free Gold source. For every friend you invite who progresses:
- Villages 2–9 of your invitee: **20 Gold each** (up to 8 invitees × 8 villages = 160 Gold per person)
- Village 10 of your invitee: **40 Gold**
- Works up to **10 different invitees** → up to **2,000 Gold total**

If you have even one friend starting the server, invite them. The Gold from a single active invitee covers the Gold Club and then some.

### 2. Sell Silver at the Auction House
**200 Silver = 1 Gold**. Silver comes from hero adventures and selling unwanted items.

> 💡 Sell consumables like Cages and Ointments **early** — prices drop once Tier 2/3 items flood the market.

### 3. Starter Offer
A one-time low-cost Gold pack appears a few hours after you start. It's cheap and appears only once. Worth considering even for F2P players if it fits your budget — but not required.

### 4. Transfer Gold Between Servers
Unused Gold from a finished server can be transferred to the next one. When a server ends, check your email for a transfer link.

## What to Spend Gold On (Priority Order for F2P)

### 🥇 Priority 1 — Gold Club (200 Gold, one-time, lasts the whole server)

**Buy this first.** It's the best value purchase in the game and never expires on that server.

Key features you actually need as F2P:
- **Trade Routes** — automatically ship surplus resources from feeders to anvil, every hour, without you clicking anything
- **Farm List** — send raids to dozens of inactives with one click
- **Troop Evasion** — evacuate your troops before an incoming attack lands

Without Gold Club, managing trade routes manually across 4+ villages is a full-time job. With it, your economy runs while you sleep.

### 🥈 Priority 2 — Travian Plus (quality of life)

Travian Plus gives you a building queue (queue multiple upgrades without waiting), the full-screen map, and attack notifications in village lists. Worth activating once you have Gold Club covered.

Key F2P-relevant features:
- **Waiting loop** — queue several building tasks in sequence
- **Central village overview** — manage all villages from one screen
- **Attack notifications** — see incoming attacks in the village list at a glance

### 🥉 Priority 3 — Resource Production Bonuses (5 Gold each, 7 days)

+25% to one resource type for 7 days. As F2P, only activate these if:
- You have Gold to spare after Club + Plus
- You're in a critical building phase (e.g. racing to get fields to L10 on the anvil)
- You activate the **25% bonus from watching an ad first** — that's free and stacks

> ⚠️ The free ad-based +25% production bonus at server start is permanent and costs nothing. Always activate it.

### ❌ Skip — NPC Merchant, Instant Completion

These cost Gold per use and drain your reserves fast. As F2P, avoid them. Plan your builds around your natural resource income instead.

## Silver — How to Use It

Silver is earned from hero adventures and selling items at the Auction House.

### What to buy with Silver

| Item | Why |
|------|-----|
| **Horse** (any tier) | Hero needs a horse to raid effectively. Get one early. |
| **Helmet of Awareness** | +% hero XP gain — hero levels up faster, meaning more stat points and faster healing |
| **Boots of the Warrior** (defender) | Faster hero movement = faster defensive response |
| **Ointments** | Heal hero after dangerous adventures or lost battles |
| **Books of Wisdom** | Respec hero attribute points — essential when switching from Strength to Resource Production |

### What to sell for Silver

| Item | When to sell |
|------|-------------|
| **Cages** | Sell early — prices drop as the server matures |
| **Ointments (extra)** | Keep a stash of ~5, sell the rest |
| **Low-tier duplicates** | If you already have a Tier 2 helmet, sell the Tier 1 |
| **Scrolls** | High value early, sell if you don't need them |

> 💡 Watch auction prices before listing. Check what similar items sold for in the last 24 hours — don't undercut yourself.

## Hero Items — What to Equip

### Early Game (first village phase)

| Slot | Item | Why |
|------|------|-----|
| Helmet | Helmet of Awareness | Faster XP → faster level-ups → instant full heal on level-up |
| Mount | Any horse | Speed for raiding oases |
| Armour | Light armour (damage reduction) | Survivability on adventures |
| Consumable | Ointments | Emergency heal |

### Mid Game (multiple villages, defender role)

| Slot | Item | Why |
|------|------|-----|
| Helmet | Helmet of Awareness or Gladiator Helmet | XP gain or CP bonus |
| Mount | Thoroughbred or Warhorse | Faster response to def calls |
| Boots | Boots of the Warrior | Speed to reach defenders faster |
| Armour | Segmented/Scale armour | Survivability |
| Consumable | Ointments + Bandages | Combat healing |

### Peace Time ("I'm home" set)

| Slot | Item | Why |
|------|------|-----|
| Helmet | Gladiator Helmet | Passive CP generation while hero sits home |

## Auction House Tips

- Bid using the **maximum bid** system — set your true max and let the system bid for you
- If a bid is placed in the last **5 minutes**, the timer resets to 5 minutes — don't assume you've won until it's over
- You can have up to **5 active sales** at once
- Consumables can be listed in stacks (5, 10, 30, or 50 units) — stack them for convenience
- Unsold items are bought by the system at starting price — don't set starting price too high
