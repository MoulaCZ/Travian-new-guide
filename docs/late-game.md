# Late Game & Endgame

Late game begins when the **Endgame (Wonder of the World)** phase approaches. The server's map shrinks, artefacts appear, and alliances compete to build the Wonder of the World (WW) to level 100.

---

## Endgame Phases

### Phase 1: Artefacts Appear (~month 4–6)
- Artefacts spawn on the map at fixed locations
- Alliances **race to capture** them — they provide powerful bonuses
- Your alliance will coordinate who captures which artefacts

### Phase 2: Plans Released (~month 8–10)
- Building plans for the **Wonder of the World** appear in the game
- Alliances must capture **all four plans** to unlock WW construction
- Massive coordinated attacks happen here

### Phase 3: Wonder of the World Construction
- One alliance builds the WW village to level **100**
- Other alliances try to destroy it with hammers
- The **first alliance to reach level 100 wins the server**

---

## Artefacts

Artefacts are powerful items that give bonuses to the controlling village.

### Types of Artefacts

| Artefact | Bonus |
|----------|-------|
| **Unique Artefact** | Server-wide bonus (very powerful, one per server) |
| **Large Artefact** | Alliance-wide bonus |
| **Small Artefact** | Village-specific bonus |

### Common Artefact Effects

| Effect | What it does |
|--------|-------------|
| **Storage** | Allows hero to carry 3x more resources |
| **Architect** | Reduces building costs |
| **Trainer** | Faster troop training |
| **Fool** | Deceives enemy scouts (they see fake numbers) |
| **Boots** | Troops move faster |
| **Diet** | Troops consume less crop |
| **Eyes** | Extended scouting range |
| **Confusion** | Makes enemy scouts unreliable |

> 💡 The **Diet artefact** is one of the most sought-after — halving crop consumption lets you maintain a much larger army.

## The Endgame Meta: Hammers and Anvils

### Hammer (Offensive Army)
A **hammer** is a massive offensive army built in one village with the sole purpose of attacking enemies.

**What makes a good hammer:**
- Large number of high-attack units (Imperian, Axemen, Teutonic Knights)
- Catapults to destroy buildings
- Battering Rams to destroy walls
- Hidden from enemies (use Fool artefact, avoid scouting)

**Key hammer stat:** A competitive late-game hammer has **100,000+ attack points** and **500+ catapults**.

### Anvil (Defensive Army)
An **anvil** is a defensive army concentrated in one village to absorb attacks.

**What makes a good anvil:**
- Massive numbers of high-defense units (Praetorians, Phalanx, Druidriders)
- Strong wall
- Usually placed in the WW holder village or key alliance villages

**Key anvil stat:** A good anvil has **500,000+ defense points** against both infantry and cavalry.

---

## Building the WW

If your alliance is the WW holder:
- The WW village is special — it has a unique building that gets upgraded to 100
- Everyone in the alliance contributes resources
- The WW building accelerates in cost — higher levels require enormous resources
- Defense is critical — enemy hammers will target the WW constantly

If you're **not** the WW holder but in the same alliance:
- Feed resources to the WW village via trade routes
- Help defend — reinforce the WW village with your anvil
- Coordinate attacks to weaken competing WW villages

---

## Destroying Enemy WWs

If you're attacking an enemy WW:
- Time your hammer to hit when enemy defense is weakest
- Coordinate multiple hammers to hit simultaneously (within seconds)
- Target the **WW building** with catapults to set it back levels
- Even setting back by 5–10 levels can delay them enough to lose

---

## Final Tips for Endgame

1. **Communicate constantly** — endgame is 90% coordination, 10% individual play
2. **Don't waste your hammer** — only attack with full preparation and alliance coordination
3. **Protect your WW** — reinforcements from the entire alliance should be stationed there
4. **Resource supply chains** — automate trade routes to keep WW building non-stop
5. **Fake attacks** — send small troops to trigger enemy defense, draining their reinforcements
6. **Hero usage** — a high-level hero with good equipment significantly boosts hammer effectiveness

## Server Outcome

| Place | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **1st** | Alliance that builds WW to level 100 first — wins the server |
| **Top 3** | Usually celebrated in the community |
| **Others** | Rankings by population, attack, defense points |

A server run is typically **10–12 months** from start to finish on 1x speed.
