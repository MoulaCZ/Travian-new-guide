# Mid Game

Mid game starts roughly when your first village is well-developed and you're thinking about **expansion** — settling or conquering a second village. This is the most complex phase of the game.

---

## When Does Mid Game Start?

There's no hard date, but typically **days 14–60** depending on activity. Signs you're in mid game:
- Your capital village is mostly built up
- You have 100+ troops
- You're ready to settle a second village
- Your resource production covers troop upkeep with surplus

---

## Goal 1: Second Village

Settling a second village is the most important mid-game milestone. Two villages = double the income, more flexibility, specialized roles.

### Requirements
- **3 Settlers** (train in Barracks after researching)
- **Palace** (level 1) OR **Residence** (level 1) in your current village
- Resources to found the village (~4,400 total)
- A **valid settlement spot** on the map (unoccupied valley)

### Where to settle?

**Best options (priority order):**
1. **15-crop oasis valley** — crop-only tile, ideal for a future army village (produces massive crop)
2. **9-crop valley** — strong crop production, good for a second resource village
3. **Standard 4-4-4-6 valley** — balanced, fine for a general-purpose village

> 💡 Your **capital village** (the one you started with) should eventually be your **crop village** (15-crop or 9-crop). Your second village can then focus on production and army.

### 15-crop strategy
Many experienced players settle a **15-crop village as their second village** early. Even if it has low production at first, developing it into a massive crop source later enables huge armies.

---

## Goal 2: Develop Your Army

Mid game is when you transition from "survival" troops to **purpose-built units**.

### Roles your army can fill

| Role | Description | Units |
|------|-------------|-------|
| **Hammer** | Large offensive army for attacking/raiding | High-attack units: Imperian, Axemen, TK, Haeduan |
| **Anvil** | Defensive army staying in villages | Praetorians, Phalanx, Druidriders, Spearmen |
| **Raider** | Fast, mobile raiders hitting many targets | Theutates Thunder, Equites Imperatoris |

Talk to your alliance to understand **what role they need** from you.

### Smithy upgrades
Upgrade your main unit in the Smithy (attack/defense bonus):
- Each level = +1.5% bonus
- Prioritize your **primary offensive unit** for attack upgrades
- Defensive upgrades matter less (more cost-efficient to just train more troops)

---

## Goal 3: Oases

Oases give **bonus production** (+25% or +50%) to your village. Each village can occupy up to 3 oases.

### How to occupy an oasis
1. Build **Hero's Mansion** to level 10 (1 oasis), 15 (2 oases), 20 (3 oases)
2. Send your hero with troops to **attack** the oasis (animals defend it)
3. Once captured, the bonus applies permanently

### Oasis types
| Type | Bonus |
|------|-------|
| 25% Wood | +25% lumber production |
| 25% Clay | +25% clay production |
| 25% Iron | +25% iron production |
| 25% Crop | +25% crop production |
| 50% Crop | +50% crop production |
| Mixed (e.g. 25% Wood + 25% Clay) | Combined bonus |

> 💡 Crop oases are the most valuable. Priority: capture crop oases for your main village.

---

## Goal 4: Alliance Coordination

Mid game is when alliance play becomes important.

### What to coordinate
- **Shared defense** — reinforce each other when attacked
- **Joint attacks** — coordinated strikes on enemy players/alliances
- **Nap/diplomacy** — non-aggression pacts with nearby alliances
- **WWk/WW planning** — start discussing endgame roles (who will build WW artefacts)

### Your contribution
- Share your **crop surplus** with allies who have big hammers
- Participate in **alliance wars** — report enemy activity
- Be responsive — a key weakness of new players is going offline for too long

## Building Priorities in Mid Game

| Building | Why |
|----------|-----|
| **Main Building** → level 10+ | Faster construction, prerequisite for many buildings |
| **Academy** + **Smithy** → upgrade your main unit | Stronger troops |
| **Stable** | Train cavalry |
| **Workshop** | Train siege weapons (Rams, Catapults) |
| **Treasury** | Required later for Artefacts in endgame |
| **Trade Office** | Faster merchant speed, more capacity |
| **Hero's Mansion** | More oasis slots |

---

## Managing Multiple Villages

Once you have 2+ villages:
- Set up **trade routes** between villages (Resources → Marketplace → Trade Routes)
- Specialize each village (one for resources, one for army)
- Keep defenses in both villages
- Track crop usage carefully — armies grow fast

---

## Common Mid Game Mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Settling in a bad location | Research valley quality before sending settlers |
| Not upgrading Smithy | Even +10% attack matters at scale |
| Ignoring oases | A 25% crop oasis is free income forever |
| No second village by day 30 | Plan earlier, save resources |
| Growing army without matching crop | Always expand crop first, then army |
