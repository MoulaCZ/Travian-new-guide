# Military

Understanding how combat works in Travian is essential — a mismanaged army means wasted resources and lost troops.

---

## Basic Combat Concepts

### Attack vs. Defense
Every unit has **attack** and **defense** values (separate values vs. infantry and cavalry).

- When you attack: your **attack** is compared against the defender's **defense**
- Combat formula is not linear — a 2x defense doesn't always equal 2x fewer losses

### Attacker always loses more
In Travian, **attacking is inherently costly**. Even a winning attack results in troop losses. This is by design — defenders have the advantage of walls, hero defense bonuses, etc.

> 💡 Never attack unless you've scouted and calculated you'll win with acceptable losses.

---

## Types of Military Actions

| Action | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Attack (raid)** | Steal resources from another village |
| **Attack (destroy)** | Send catapults to destroy buildings |
| **Reinforcement** | Send troops to defend another village |
| **Scout** | Send scouts to spy on enemy village |
| **Settle** | Send settlers to found a new village |
| **Conquer** | Use a Chief/Chieftain/Senator to reduce loyalty |

## Raiding

Raiding is the primary way to supplement your resource income, especially early game.

### How raids work
1. Send troops to an enemy/inactive village
2. Troops arrive, fight any defenders (if present)
3. Winning troops carry resources back to your village
4. Each unit has a **carry capacity**

### Carry capacity by unit type (examples)

| Unit | Carry capacity |
|------|---------------|
| Clubswinger | 60 |
| Legionnaire | 40 |
| Theutates Thunder | 75 |
| Equites Imperatoris | 100 |

### Farm List
Use the **Farm List** (Rally Point → Farm List) to manage and automate raids:
- Add inactive villages you raid regularly
- Send all farms with one click ("Send All")
- Sort by last sent, distance, or resources stolen

> 💡 Farm list management is one of the most important skills for efficient resource income. Build it up from day 1.

### Finding farms
- Grey player names on the map = inactive/low activity
- Use the **Statistics** page to find players with no recent activity
- Ideal farm: no troops, full resources, small population

## Scouting

Scouts reveal:
- **Troop count and types** in the target village (if your scouts survive)
- **Resource amounts** in the village

### Scout mechanics
- Both attacker and defender send scouts to "fight"
- More scouts = higher chance of successful intel
- If defender has no scouts, your scout always succeeds
- If defender has scouts, they "cancel each other out" — you need more than they have

### When to scout
- **Always scout** before sending a real attack on an active player
- Don't need to scout known inactive farms
- Scout enemy WW village regularly in endgame

## Conquering Villages

To take over another player's village, you must **reduce its loyalty to 0**.

### Loyalty mechanics
- Every village starts at 100 loyalty
- Each Chief/Chieftain/Senator attack reduces loyalty by **20–30 points** (random)
- You need **multiple Chief attacks** to conquer
- Loyalty regenerates over time (slowly)

### Conquest process
1. Destroy wall and troops in the target village (send a hammer first)
2. Send Chief/Senator in follow-up attacks to reduce loyalty
3. When loyalty hits 0, the village switches to your control
4. You can then rename and develop it

> ⚠️ You cannot conquer a village with 0 population — be careful not to destroy all buildings before conquering.

## Wall Effects

Each nation has a different wall type with different defense bonuses.

| Nation | Wall Type | Defense Bonus (max level) |
|--------|-----------|--------------------------|
| Romans | City Wall | +70% |
| Gauls | Palisade | +55% |
| Teutons | Earth Wall | +40% |

- The bonus applies to **all troops defending** the village
- Destroying the wall with Battering Rams removes the bonus
- Always send Rams when attacking defended villages

## Hero in Combat

Your hero is a powerful unit that can dramatically change battle outcomes.

- Hero can be sent with attacks or kept home for defense
- Hero gains experience and levels up with combat
- Hero has **regenerating health** — if lost in battle, they respawn (but lose items)
- Hero items give attack/defense bonuses

### Hero strategy
- Early game: send with every attack (boosts combat)
- Mid game: equip for max attack (sword, armor)
- Late game: keep alive — a dead hero means item drops

## Combat Tips

1. **Scout before every non-trivial attack** — don't lose troops to hidden defenses
2. **Send Rams against walls** — a wall at level 20 gives +70% defense; remove it
3. **Use catapults to target specific buildings** (Main Building slows construction, Granary starves troops)
4. **Coordinate simultaneous attacks** — multiple hammers landing within seconds of each other prevent the defender from reinforcing
5. **Don't split your hammer** — one large attack is much more effective than several small ones
6. **Fake attacks** — send a single unit as a "fake" to confuse the enemy about your real attack timing

---

## Attack Timing & Wave Techniques

In serious PvP, precise timing matters:

### Wave attack
- Send multiple waves from the same village within seconds of each other
- Rams arrive first (if timed correctly) to destroy wall before main army arrives
- Catapults arrive last to clean up buildings

### Simultaneous multi-village attacks
- Coordinate with alliance members to all hit the same target at the same time
- Even 2-second differences can allow the defender to react
- Use in-game clock and tools to synchronize
