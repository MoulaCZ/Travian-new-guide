# Gauls (Gallic Tribe)

## Overview

Gauls are the **fastest and most defensive** nation. Their units are quick, making them excellent for raids and rapid reinforcement. They are considered beginner-friendly because of strong early defense — enemies will think twice before attacking you.

**Difficulty:** Easy  
**Role:** Defense, support, fast raiding  
**Recommended for:** New players who want safety, or experienced players coordinating defense

---

## Unique Mechanics

- **Trapper building:** Gauls can build Traps that automatically capture attacking troops — passive defense.
- **Fastest units:** Gallic cavalry is the fastest in the game, great for scouting and reinforcing allies quickly.
- **Dual-resource starting bonus:** Gauls start with an extra Cranny that hides more resources from attackers.

| Building | Notes |

> 💡 The **Trapper** is extremely powerful in early game. Attackers who don't scout first will lose troops to it. Build and fill it with traps as soon as possible.

## Recommended Strategy

### Early game (Days 1–14)
1. Build Trapper immediately — fill it with traps. This deters early attackers.
2. Train Phalanx for defense and some Theutates Thunder for raiding.
3. Use fast cavalry to raid many inactive neighbors efficiently.
4. Upgrade Cranny to protect your resources.

### Mid game
- Druidriders make excellent reinforcements for allies — you can respond to attacks faster than any other nation.
- Consider becoming your alliance's **defensive specialist** — mass Phalanx and Druidriders.
- Build second village on a good resource tile.

### Late game
- Gauls are often the **anvil** (defensive wall) in coordinated endgame attacks.
- Theutates Thunder "scouts" (armies of fast cavalry) can track enemy movements.
- Haeduans form a strong offensive hammer if you go full offense.

---

## Tips

- Gauls are the hardest nation to lose early — Trappers + Phalanx make attacking you expensive.
- Always keep traps full in the Trapper. Check daily.
- Druidriders are often underrated — they're one of the most cost-effective defensive units in the game.
- Use Theutates Thunder for **raiding lists** — hit many villages in fast succession.
