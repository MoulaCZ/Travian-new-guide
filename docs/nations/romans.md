# Romans (Imperium Romanum)

## Overview

Romans are the most **balanced nation** in Travian. They have a unique advantage: buildings and troop training can happen **simultaneously** (one construction queue for buildings, one for troops). This makes them efficient and forgiving for new players.

**Difficulty:** Medium  
**Role:** Balanced — suitable for offense, defense, or support  
**Recommended for:** New players who want a structured, efficient start

---

## Unique Mechanics

- **Dual queue:** You can train troops in the Barracks/Stable while upgrading buildings at the same time. Other nations must choose one or the other at low smithy/main building levels.
- **Faster hero regeneration:** Roman hero recovers health faster than other nations.
- **Legionnaire wall (City Wall):** Provides strong static defense bonus.

---

## Buildings

Romans have access to all standard buildings plus Roman-specific ones:

| Building | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| **City Wall** | Highest defense bonus of all walls |
| **Horse Drinking Trough** | Reduces crop consumption for cavalry — very important |
| **Palace** | Required to settle/conquer new villages |

> 💡 Build the **Horse Drinking Trough** early if you plan to use cavalry. It significantly cuts upkeep costs.

## Units

### Infantry

| Unit | Attack | Defense (inf) | Defense (cav) | Crop | Cost (res) |
|------|--------|---------------|---------------|------|------------|
| **Legionnaire** | 40 | 35 | 50 | 1 | Medium |
| **Praetorian** | 30 | 65 | 35 | 1 | Medium |
| **Imperian** | 70 | 40 | 25 | 1 | Medium |

- **Legionnaire** — Versatile all-around unit, good for early raids
- **Praetorian** — Best defensive infantry in the game, cheap to maintain
- **Imperian** — Strong attacker, backbone of Roman offensive armies

### Cavalry

| Unit | Attack | Defense (inf) | Defense (cav) | Crop | Cost |
|------|--------|---------------|---------------|------|------|
| **Equites Legati** | 0 | 20 | 10 | 2 | Low |
| **Equites Imperatoris** | 120 | 65 | 50 | 3 | High |
| **Equites Caesaris** | 180 | 80 | 105 | 4 | Very high |

- **Equites Legati** — Scout unit (attack = 0, detects enemy scouts)
- **Equites Imperatoris** — Fast raiding cavalry, good attack
- **Equites Caesaris** — Strongest Roman unit, expensive but devastating

### Special

| Unit | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **Battering Ram** | Destroys enemy walls |
| **Fire Catapult** | Destroys enemy buildings |
| **Senator** | Lowers enemy loyalty for conquest |
| **Settler** | Founds new villages (3 needed) |

## Recommended Strategy

### Early game (Days 1–14)
1. Max out resource fields using quest rewards.
2. Train Legionnaires — start raiding inactive players early.
3. Upgrade Main Building to level 5+ (speeds up all construction).
4. Build Marketplace for trading.
5. Research Imperian in Academy when you have resources.

### Mid game
- Transition your army to Imperiani + Equites Imperatoris.
- Build a second village as soon as possible (need 3 Settlers + Palace/Residence).
- Focus on 15-crop or 9-crop village for your capital.

### Late game
- Romans make excellent **hammer** (offensive army) holders.
- Equites Caesaris are among the strongest units in the game.
- Coordinate large-scale attacks with your alliance.

---

## Tips

- Romans are slower to start than Teutons (no cheap units), but catch up quickly due to dual queue.
- Praetorians are one of the best defensive units — useful even as a non-defender to contribute to alliance defense.
- Always build the Horse Drinking Trough before massing cavalry.
