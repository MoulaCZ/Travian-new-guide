# Teutons (Teutonic Tribe)

## Overview

Teutons are the **most aggressive and raiding-focused** nation. Their units are cheap and fast to produce, making them deadly early raiders. However, Teutons require active, consistent play — if you don't raid, you fall behind. They are not recommended for inactive or casual players.

**Difficulty:** Hard  
**Role:** Raid, offense, early domination  
**Recommended for:** Experienced players, active players who can check the game multiple times per day

---

## Unique Mechanics

- **Cheapest units:** Clubswingers are the cheapest attackers in the game — you can mass them quickly.
- **Cranny plundering:** Teutons can steal a portion of resources **even from hidden Cranny storage**, which other nations cannot.
- **Brewery:** Unique building that temporarily boosts army attack at the cost of crop.
- **Makeshift Wall:** Weakest wall in the game — Teutons rely on offense, not wall defense.

## Recommended Strategy

### Early game (Days 1–14)

> ⚠️ Teuton early game is about **raiding, raiding, raiding**. Without raids, your economy will lag behind Romans and Gauls who can use their building queues more efficiently.

1. Train Clubswingers **immediately and non-stop**.
2. Scout nearby inactive villages and start raiding within the first few hours.
3. Build Barracks to level 3–5 quickly — more Barracks = more training queues (advanced tactic).
4. Upgrade resource fields when raid income allows.
5. Keep raids going 24/7 — use the auto-repeat raid feature on reliable inactive farms.

**Key insight:** Your Clubswingers raid Crannies partially — even protected resources are not 100% safe from you. Use this to bully neighbors and fund your expansion.

### Mid game
- Transition from Clubswingers to **Axemen** as your primary unit.
- Begin training Teutonic Knights once you can sustain crop production.
- Expand aggressively — Teutons benefit greatly from a 15-crop second village.
- Consider the **Brewery** as a priority for endgame preparation.

### Late game
- The classic Teuton endgame role is building a **massive offensive hammer** (Axemen + Teutonic Knights + Catapults).
- Brewery at high levels + WW attack = one of the most feared combos in the game.
- Coordinate with alliance for synchronized WW attacks.

---

## Tips

- Start raiding within the **first 2–3 hours** of the game. Don't wait.
- Build multiple Barracks (up to 3) in early game to mass-produce Clubswingers faster.
- Clubswingers are **not worth upgrading in Smithy** — invest smithy upgrades in Axemen instead.
- Don't neglect Spearmen — they protect against cavalry raids on your village while your army is out.
- Farm list management is crucial — use Travian's built-in farm list to organize and automate raids.
- Teutons are punishing if you miss a few hours of raiding — set up reliable inactive farm targets early.
