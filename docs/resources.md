# Resources

Resources are the foundation of everything in Travian. Without a solid economy, you cannot build, train, or maintain an army.

---

## The Four Resources

| Resource | Produced by | Used for |
|----------|------------|---------|
| **Lumber (Wood)** | Woodcutter | Buildings (primary) |
| **Clay** | Clay Pit | Buildings (primary) |
| **Iron** | Iron Mine | Troops, buildings |
| **Crop** | Cropland | Troop upkeep (consumed every hour) |

> ⚠️ **Crop is unique** — it is consumed continuously by your troops. If crop production goes negative, troops start to starve and die one by one.

---

## Resource Fields

Each village has a fixed number of resource fields (always 18). The distribution varies by valley type.

### Valley Types

| Valley Type | Lumber | Clay | Iron | Crop | Best for |
|-------------|--------|------|------|------|---------|
| **4-4-4-6** | 4 | 4 | 4 | 6 | General purpose (starting village) |
| **3-4-5-4** | 3 | 4 | 5 | 4 | Iron-focused |
| **4-5-3-4** | 4 | 5 | 3 | 4 | Clay-focused |
| **5-3-4-4** | 5 | 3 | 4 | 4 | Lumber-focused |
| **1-1-1-15** | 1 | 1 | 1 | 15 | Army village (15-crop) |
| **3-3-3-9** | 3 | 3 | 3 | 9 | Army/hybrid (9-crop) |

### 15-Crop and 9-Crop Villages
These are the most valuable village types in the game. With a 15-crop village as your capital:
- Upgrade all 15 crop fields to max level
- A single 15-crop village at max level produces **~30,000+ crop/hour** — enough to feed enormous armies
- This is why experienced players always try to settle a 15-crop early

## Oases

Oases give **percentage bonuses** to production of a specific resource type.

- Each village can occupy **up to 3 oases**
- Requires **Hero's Mansion** at level 10 / 15 / 20
- You capture oases by attacking them with your hero + troops

### Priority
1. Crop oases (25% or 50%) — most impactful
2. Whatever resource you're short on
3. Mixed oases — decent if pure oases aren't nearby

---

## Storage

Resources are stored in **Warehouse** (wood/clay/iron) and **Granary** (crop).

| Building | Stores |
|----------|--------|
| **Warehouse** | Lumber, Clay, Iron |
| **Granary** | Crop |

- Both need to be upgraded regularly as your production increases
- If storage is full, production **stops** — you're wasting resources
- Multiple warehouses/granaries can be built in the same village

> 💡 A full warehouse is a warning sign — either upgrade it or spend the resources immediately.

---

## Cranny (Hiding Resources)

The **Cranny** protects a small amount of resources from being raided.

- Level 1 Cranny: ~200 of each resource hidden
- Level 10 Cranny: ~2,000 of each resource hidden
- Gauls get an extra free Cranny at start

**Important:** Teutons can steal **a portion of Cranny-protected resources** — no one else can.

**When to build Cranny:**
- Early game, before your army is strong
- When you're being raided frequently
- Not necessary once you have strong defense or active alliance protection

## Trading

Use the **Marketplace** to trade resources with other players.

### Trade mechanics
- Each merchant carries a fixed amount (depends on tribe)
- Merchants take time to travel — longer distance = longer travel time
- You can set **trade routes** (recurring automatic trades between your own villages)

### When to trade
- You're overproducing one resource and short on another
- Alliance members need emergency resources
- Sending resources to a new village you just settled

### Exchange rate tip
The server has a **free market** — exchange rates are set by players. Common fair rates:
- 1:1 for wood/clay/iron among each other
- Crop is usually cheaper (1 crop = 0.5–0.8 of other resources) unless near endgame

## Crop Management

Crop management is the most important resource skill to master.

### Crop balance formula
```
Crop balance = Crop production (per hour) - Crop consumption (per hour)
```

Consumption comes from:
- **Troops in your village** (each unit eats 1–4 crop/hour)
- **Troops you've sent out** still consume crop
- **Hero** consumes crop

### Warning signs
| Sign | Action |
|------|--------|
| Granary always full | Upgrade granary or train more troops |
| Negative crop balance | Upgrade crop fields immediately or dismiss excess troops |
| Troops dying | Critical — you have negative crop and empty granary |

### Crop checklist
- Always have positive crop balance (production > consumption)
- Build crop fields to level 5+ before massing troops
- For large armies: 15-crop village is essential in late game
